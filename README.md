## The Mural That Caught Fire

A colossal phoenix tears itself free from a painted mural on a city wall
at night. The painted bird bursts out of the flat brick into real fire,
wings spread huge across the frame, molten gold and deep crimson feathers
glowing against a dark ink-blue sky. Paint cracks and flakes fly, embers
spiral upward and turn into stars, and far below on the wet street a tiny
crowd reaches up in awe.

The image is a JPEG, 683 × 1024 pixels, 294,446 bytes, with one clear
focal point: the phoenix's blazing head and outstretched wings.

## Why it should excite the judge

- **A jolt of awe:** one look is a bird the size of a building tearing
  itself out of a wall into living flame.
- **Surprise:** a flat painted mural becoming real fire is an idea of its
  own, not a stock explosion.
- **Momentum:** the diagonal wings, the rising embers turning into stars,
  and the cracking paint all drive toward the same bursting moment.
- **Craft:** clean confident brushwork, no text, no watermark, no garbled
  shapes.
- **Reads small:** at 300 pixels across the phoenix silhouette, the
  blazing head, and the tiny crowd below are still clear.

## How it runs

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` into the output directory the harness names
with `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`), then checks the copy is
byte-identical and really a JPEG or PNG by its first bytes.
